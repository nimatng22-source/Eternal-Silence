Eternal-Silence

A Global Initiative for Peace, Civilian Protection, and Responsible AI

Human life is more important than the divisions that separate us.

Eternal-Silence is an independent initiative dedicated to promoting peace, protecting civilians, reducing human suffering, and encouraging the responsible development and use of artificial intelligence.

Our goal is to build a lasting foundation for cooperation, knowledge, transparency, and practical solutions that support a safer future for humanity.

Our Mission

We believe that technology, knowledge, and international cooperation should serve humanity.

Eternal-Silence aims to:

- Promote peace and peaceful cooperation.
- Support the protection of civilians and vulnerable communities.
- Encourage responsible and ethical artificial intelligence.
- Share reliable educational and humanitarian information.
- Build a project that can continue beyond any single individual.

Our Core Principles

- Human Dignity: Every human life deserves respect and protection.
- Peace: Dialogue and cooperation must come before violence.
- Civilian Protection: The safety of civilians is a fundamental priority.
- Responsible AI: Technology must respect human rights and remain under meaningful human oversight.
- Transparency: Decisions and progress should be documented honestly.
- Independence: The initiative must remain focused on its humanitarian mission.
- Continuity: Future contributors should be able to understand and continue the project.

How to Contribute

Everyone who shares our principles can help.

You do not need to be a programmer. You can contribute through research, documentation, translation, constructive feedback, and responsible ideas.

Please read "CONTRIBUTING.md" (CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting contributions.

Our Commitment

Eternal-Silence does not promote violence, hatred, discrimination, or violations of human rights.

We recognize that lasting peace requires responsibility, cooperation, patience, and continuous effort.

This project is a long-term commitment to humanity and future generations.
