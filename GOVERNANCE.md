
Eternal-Silence

Governance and Project Continuity

1. Purpose

This document explains how Eternal-Silence should be managed, how decisions should be made, and how the project can continue responsibly over time.

Its purpose is to protect the project's mission, independence, transparency, and long-term sustainability.

2. Foundational Authority

The project's foundational principles are defined in MISSION.md.

All project activities, decisions, and future changes should remain consistent with its commitment to peace, human dignity, civilian protection, and responsible AI.

No individual or group should use the project to promote violence, discrimination, or violations of human rights.

3. Decision-Making

Important project decisions should follow a transparent and documented process.

Decisions should:

- Be consistent with the mission.
- Consider ethical and safety implications.
- Be supported by reliable information.
- Allow reasonable discussion and review.
- Include an explanation of their purpose.

Major changes should not be made without appropriate review.

4. Roles and Responsibilities

As the project grows, responsibilities may be shared among contributors.

Possible roles include:

- Project Maintainers: Help organize the repository and maintain project documentation.
- Contributors: Suggest improvements, research topics, and submit changes.
- Reviewers: Examine proposed changes for accuracy, safety, and alignment with the mission.
- Advisors: Provide specialized knowledge and independent recommendations.

These roles should be assigned transparently and should not grant unlimited authority.

5. Checks and Accountability

People responsible for reviewing or approving changes should act in the project's best interests.

Important decisions should be documented.

Where practical, sensitive or major decisions should receive independent review.

Conflicts of interest should be disclosed.

No person should be exempt from reasonable accountability.

6. Project Independence

Eternal-Silence should remain independent of political parties, military organizations, and commercial interests that conflict with its mission.

Cooperation is welcome when it respects the project's principles and independence.

No external organization should be allowed to secretly control the project's direction.

7. Changes to Foundational Documents

Changes to MISSION.md and other foundational documents should be carefully reviewed.

Proposed changes should include:

- A clear explanation.
- The reason for the change.
- Potential benefits and risks.
- An opportunity for discussion.

The original mission should not be changed casually or without transparent justification.

8. Security and Access

Access to project resources should be granted according to responsibility and necessity.

Maintainers should:

- Protect account credentials.
- Use appropriate access controls.
- Review permissions periodically.
- Avoid sharing private information.
- Respond responsibly to security concerns.

No passwords, private keys, or access tokens should be stored in public project files.

9. Continuity and Succession

The project must not depend entirely on its founder.

To support continuity:

- Keep important information documented.
- Maintain clear contribution procedures.
- Preserve the project's history.
- Encourage multiple responsible maintainers.
- Document essential responsibilities.
- Provide a transparent process for transferring duties.

Any future transfer of responsibility should respect the mission and be documented.

10. Disagreements and Conflict Resolution

Disagreements should be handled respectfully.

Contributors should first attempt constructive discussion.

If a disagreement cannot be resolved, an independent review may be requested where appropriate.

Harassment, threats, and discrimination are unacceptable.

11. Transparency and Public Reporting

Major governance decisions and significant project changes should be documented publicly whenever doing so does not create security or privacy risks.

The project should communicate honestly about its achievements, limitations, and challenges.

12. Periodic Review

This governance document should be reviewed periodically as the project develops.

Improvements should strengthen transparency, accountability, safety, and continuity without abandoning the foundational mission.

13. Final Commitment

Eternal-Silence belongs to its mission, not to the permanent control of any single individual.

Its future should be guided by cooperation, responsibility, human dignity, and a shared commitment to peace.

Eternal-Silence — Preserving the mission beyond any one person.
