Humanitarian Resources and Civilian Protection Guide

Status: Initial draft
Version: 0.1
Review date: 2026-11-20

Purpose

This guide helps people find reliable resources about humanitarian assistance, civilian protection, health emergencies, and support for refugees and displaced people.

In its first phase, this project only shares public information and links to official sources. It does not provide direct emergency assistance.

Safety Principles

- Information must be checked against reliable sources.
- Unverified information must not be presented as fact.
- Personal and confidential information about vulnerable people must not be published.
- The last review date must be recorded.
- This guide does not replace emergency, medical, or legal services.
- UNHCR services are free of charge. Do not pay unofficial intermediaries for UNHCR services. Check contact details and service information only through the official UNHCR website.

1. Humanitarian Assistance

- United Nations OCHA: https://www.unocha.org/
- ReliefWeb: https://reliefweb.int/

2. Civilian Protection and Human Rights

- International Committee of the Red Cross: https://www.icrc.org/
- UN Human Rights Office: https://www.ohchr.org/

3. Health and Medical Services in Emergencies

- World Health Organization: https://www.who.int/
- WHO Health Emergencies: https://www.who.int/emergencies

4. Refugee and Displacement Support

- United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees: https://www.unhcr.org/
- UNHCR HELP: https://help.unhcr.org/

Information Review Process

Every published item should include:

1. Title and short description.
2. Link to the original source.
3. Name of the publishing organization.
4. Geographic coverage.
5. Publication date, when available.
6. Last review date.
7. Verification status.

Corrections

Outdated or incorrect information must be corrected or removed.

Users can report suggested corrections through an Issue in the repository. Personal or confidential information must not be included.

Our Commitment

Eternal-Silence is committed to human dignity, civilian protection, transparency, information verification, and reducing human suffering.

No person should lose their dignity or human rights because of war, crisis, or displacement.
