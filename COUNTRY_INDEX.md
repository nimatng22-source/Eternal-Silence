# Country Information Index

## Purpose

This index helps users find verified humanitarian, civilian protection, health, and refugee-support information by country.

## Important Notice

- This index is an information directory, not an emergency response service.
- Information must be checked against official sources before publication.
- The availability of services must never be assumed.
- Personal and confidential information must not be published.

## Country Pages

Country-specific information will be added gradually after verification.

## Information Required for Each Country

Every country page should include:

1. Official humanitarian resources.
2. Civilian protection resources.
3. Official health and emergency resources.
4. Official refugee and displacement support.
5. Geographic coverage.
6. Original source links.
7. Last verification date.
8. Verification status.

## Verification Status

- Verified: Checked against official sources.
- Pending: Awaiting verification.
- Outdated: Requires a new review.

## Safety

Never publish personal data, confidential details, or unverified emergency contact information.

## Project Commitment

Eternal-Silence is committed to accuracy, transparency, human dignity, and reducing human suffering.
