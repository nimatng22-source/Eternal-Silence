Eternal-Silence

Global Call for Collaboration and Participation

Humanity must never become a victim of borders, wars, or divisions.

Eternal-Silence is an independent global initiative dedicated to promoting peace, protecting civilians, reducing human suffering, and encouraging the responsible development and use of artificial intelligence.

We believe that building a safer future requires global cooperation, specialized knowledge, transparency, and shared responsibility.

Who Are We Looking For?

We welcome participation from:

- Software developers and engineers
- Artificial intelligence researchers
- Human rights and humanitarian professionals
- Medical and emergency health specialists
- Translators and language researchers
- Volunteers and individuals committed to humanitarian values

Areas of Collaboration

1. Designing and developing the project's technical infrastructure.
2. Collecting, reviewing, and verifying reliable humanitarian resources.
3. Creating tools that improve access to humanitarian information.
4. Developing responsible approaches to civilian protection.
5. Promoting the safe and ethical use of artificial intelligence.
6. Building international partnerships and cooperation.

Our Principles

- Transparency and accountability
- Respect for human dignity
- Independence and responsible cooperation
- Privacy and personal data protection
- Reliable information and verification
- Equal respect for every human life and human rights

An Invitation to Collaborate

We are looking for people who are willing to help turn a shared vision into practical, responsible, and sustainable work.

Whether your expertise is in technology, research, translation, humanitarian work, or volunteering, your contribution can help shape this initiative.

Let us build something that puts humanity above division.
