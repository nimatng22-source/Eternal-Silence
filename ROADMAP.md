
Eternal-Silence

Project Roadmap

Phase 1 — Foundation

- Establish the mission and core principles.
- Document the project's purpose and long-term vision.
- Define ethical boundaries and responsibilities.
- Create clear contribution guidelines.

Phase 2 — Research and Planning

- Research civilian protection and humanitarian needs.
- Identify reliable sources of information.
- Study responsible applications of artificial intelligence.
- Identify risks, limitations, and legal requirements.

Phase 3 — Project Development

- Design a transparent project structure.
- Develop educational resources.
- Explore safe and practical technology solutions.
- Establish documentation and review processes.

Phase 4 — Cooperation

- Welcome responsible contributors.
- Build relationships with researchers and humanitarian organizations.
- Encourage constructive feedback.
- Support collaboration across communities.

Phase 5 — Evaluation and Improvement

- Review progress regularly.
- Measure outcomes against the mission.
- Identify mistakes and improve project methods.
- Publish progress reports when appropriate.

Phase 6 — Long-Term Continuity

- Keep documentation accessible and organized.
- Reduce dependence on any single individual.
- Establish responsible project governance.
- Preserve the mission for future contributors.

Important Principles

- Human safety comes first.
- No activity should promote violence or discrimination.
- Privacy and security must be respected.
- Artificial intelligence must remain under meaningful human oversight.
- All development must follow applicable laws and ethical standards.

Current Status

The project is in its early documentation and planning stage.

The roadmap is a living document and may be updated transparently as the project develops.
